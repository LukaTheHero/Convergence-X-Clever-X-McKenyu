Convergence X Clever X McKenyu - The Convergence ER 3.0.2
v6.5 - 2026-10-03

Runs on Elden Ring Patch 1.17, The Convergence 3.0.2, Clever's Moveset
Modpack 26.2 and McKenyu's Moveset Modpack 1.4.

BACK UP YOUR SAVES before installing. Play offline.

NEW: McKenyu's Moveset Modpack 1.4 is now a required download, like
Clever's. Get it from his own Nexus page (a .rar file: keep it as it is,
the installer unpacks it). This zip no longer carries his files.

Using Infinite Durations? Pick it in the v6.5 Auto Installer. An older
Infinite Durations regulation (v6.4 or earlier) silently undoes v6.5.


=== NEW: THE AUTO INSTALLER ===

- One main zip (this one) plus the Auto Installer covers every
  combination. There are no separate zips per combination any more.
- Tick the mods you want. For mods made by other people, you download the
  file from their own Nexus page: the installer tells you which file,
  finds it in your Downloads and checks it.
- Clever's 26.2 and McKenyu's 1.4 packs are always required.
- Options: Nightreign Movement, Seamless Co-op, Lucy, Infinite Durations
  (any edition), Infinite Arrows and Bolts, ERCapacityExpansion, and
  Deflect Me Not (experimental).
- My own files (the merge's files for each option, Lucy, Infinite
  Durations, Infinite Arrows) download from my GitHub and are checked
  too.
- Seamless Co-op is only needed when you tick it. Both players need the
  same merge version and the same options.
- ERCapacityExpansion (by KamiyamaShiki0704) raises Elden Ring's animation
  limit. Off by default; the installer adds it automatically when you tick
  Deflect Me Not and Nightreign Movement together. Tested together with
  Seamless Co-op.
- Nightreign Movement 0.2 "Wylder" (by neiroxgod): download it from its
  Nexus page (mods/11174, the Convergence 3.0.2 edition) and tick it; keep
  the zip as it is, the installer finds and checks it. Settings at any
  Site of Grace, "Nightreign movement". Fall protection starts OFF.
  The Nightreign walk, run and roll animations stay out: they would replace
  every Clever and McKenyu weapon's own.
- Lucy: her armor is Chevaleresse II by yurica (from the Skyrim SE ports
  by THBG0 and by yurica, THBossGamer, Jeir and docteure), her hair is KS
  Hairdos by Kalilies and Stealthic (HDT SMP version by ousnius) and her
  body mesh is based on CBBE by Caliente and ousnius. The installer's
  credits name them.
- Deflect Me Not (by Rei Jr.), experimental: download Rei's DMN file for
  this merge from his Nexus page and tick it. Please report DMN problems to
  Rei Jr. and everything else to us.
- Run it again any time to change your picks. Uninstall from Windows'
  Apps list.


=== FIXED IN v6.5 ===

- Fang of Fading Thunder: the drawn-sword idle pose is one-handed only now.
  Two-handed and powerstanced, the idle is the standard stance, so the
  blade no longer goes into your body.
- Rekkaryujin: now also inflicts Burn, like Convergence's fire weapons.
  Its bleed stays.
- Built on McKenyu's Moveset Modpack 1.4: Yamato gets his two-handed L1
  combo; Rakuyo's heavy attack becomes its special heavy while the blood
  buff is on, and its reworked skill is in (hold = spin, tap = one slash);
  McKenyu's new Ronin's set texture is in.
- Heaven Splitter and Phoenix Caller: every bow move now fires its arrow
  as a real weapon hit that scales with the weapon, buffed or not (the
  weapon buffs still add their element on top).
- Phoenix Caller: the two-handed heavy attack fires its rain of arrows
  again (it used to teleport you and not hit). The arrow it shoots into
  the sky is flaming now, like the rain.
- Heaven Splitter: Heaven's Call cast with full FP now gives the arrow
  buff too.
- Heaven Splitter: the arrows' lightning burst uses Clever's own values
  instead of borrowing the Bolt of Gransax's.
- Phoenix Caller: the tri arrows no longer hit your summons and allies.
- Rakuyo: the two-handed skill (hold) now hits: the spin hits twice and
  the blood explosion hits everyone near you. Tap and hold both count as
  Skill hits (Shard of Alexander works).
- Rakuyo: jump attacks are McKenyu's own again; two-handed it uses the
  twin-blade jump.
- Starscourge Greatsword (Clever's): the two-handed jump attack (R1) is its
  own two-sword swing again.
- Marais Executioner's Sword (Clever's): Hunter's Stance no longer breaks
  other weapons afterwards (God's Bane guard follow-ups, Pale Knight
  Gunblade light-to-heavy).
- Great Shinobi Blade: the one-handed heavy throws the shuriken from the
  right hand again, and wall jumps work with it (Nightreign Movement).
- Tachikaze: the one-handed light combo sheathes the blade properly.
- Glaive of Rapture: roll and crouch attacks hit enemies right in front of
  you, the held skill staggers, and the skill's buff is holy.
- Fang of Fading Thunder: walking two-handed or powerstanced no longer puts
  the blade into your body. One-handed keeps the Raikiri walk.
- Nightreign Movement updated to 0.2 "Wylder" (Claw Shot, Onslaught
  Stake). With Deflect Me Not also ticked, the installer adds
  ERCapacityExpansion automatically, so Wylder fits next to DMN.

=== INSTALL ===

Easiest: run the v6.5 Auto Installer from the Files tab. It needs this
zip and walks you through everything below, for any combination.

By hand (the merge on its own):
1. Install The Convergence 3.0.2 and launch it once.
2. Download Clever's Moveset Modpack 26.2 from his Nexus page. Copy his
   parts folder and his chr\c0000_a00_hi.anibnd.dcx into your Convergence
   mod folder (keep the folders).
3. Download McKenyu's Moveset Modpack 1.4 from his Nexus page and unpack
   it. From his mod folder copy into your Convergence mod folder: the
   parts folder, param\systemparam\systemparam.parambnd.dcx,
   sd\enus\cs_c8000.bnk and sfx\sfxbnd_commoneffects_dlc01.ffxbnd.dcx
   (keep the folders).
4. In your Convergence mod\parts folder, copy wp_a_5071.partsbnd.dcx three
   times, named wp_a_5071_l.partsbnd.dcx, wp_a_17501.partsbnd.dcx and
   wp_a_17501_l.partsbnd.dcx.
5. Drop this archive's mod folder over your Convergence install,
   overwriting when asked. Do this step last.
6. Launch through your normal Convergence launcher, offline. The
   regulation.bin edit trips Easy AntiCheat.

Nightreign Movement, Seamless Co-op, Lucy, Infinite Durations, Infinite
Arrows, ERCapacityExpansion and Deflect Me Not: use the Auto Installer.

Updating from v6.0 - v6.4 by hand: do steps 3 to 5 (McKenyu's 1.4 files
replace his older ones). If you use any of the options above, update with
the Auto Installer instead.
Updating from v5.0 or older: full replacement, back up your saves first.
